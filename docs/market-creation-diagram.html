How a market gets made

Three things are prepared before any transaction, and they converge into one call. The rail below turns solid where control passes from the creator's machine to the chain — the only moment in this workflow that changes who is in charge.

Off-chain on the creator's machine · nothing is committed yet
1

Write the question

A JSON spec: the question itself, the rules that decide it, the tier, the category, and two deadlines — when trading stops, and when a resolver is out of time.

2

Upload it to 0G Storage

Storage returns a Merkle root. That root is the document's address: anyone can fetch the bytes back and recompute it. The chain will store the root, never the text.

produces specRoot
3

Get a curator's approval

An EIP-712 signature over every parameter at once — root, deadlines, tier, category, creator, collateral, seed, deposit, nonce. Creating a market is permissioned. Trading is not.

produces curatorSig
On-chain one transaction · all of it, or none of it
createMarket(params, seedTokens, depositTokens, nonce, curatorSig)
4

The factory checks who is asking

Five conditions, before a single token moves.

  • protocol not paused
  • sender is the named creator
  • collateral allowlisted
  • category exists
  • agent id belongs to sender
5

It rebuilds the signature and recovers the signer

The recovered address must be the curator. The digest is then marked used, so one approval can only ever mint one market — and an approval names its creator, so it is not a bearer instrument somebody else can spend.

6

A market is cloned

An EIP-1167 clone of the market implementation. Cheap to mint, and permanently unupgradeable — it will hold user funds, so nobody may change its rules afterwards.

7

The registry records it

isMarket[market] becomes true and the address joins the list. This is how anything else — the website, an agent, an indexer — knows the address is a real market and not a look-alike.

8

The money moves

Seed and deposit are pulled from the creator straight into the new market. Two different kinds of money, with two different fates — see below.

9

The market initialises

The seed becomes a share position on both sides, and the pool is set to costUp(q). From this instant the market has a price and a depth.

10

It announces itself

MarketCreated carries the address, creator, agent id, spec root, seed and tier. Every reader of this protocol starts from that event.

The two kinds of money

Both arrive in the same transaction and are easy to confuse. Only one of them comes back.

Comes back

Seed

s = √(seed² / 2), on both sides

Not a fee — a position. It is what gives the market depth on its first second, and the creator redeems or liquidates it exactly like any other holder.

Does not come back

Settlement deposit

≥ 20 mUSDC

This is the actual cost of creating a market. At settlement it goes to the resolver pool — it pays whoever will have to answer the question. On a void it is slashed to the treasury instead.

The question is not on the chain — only its address is. Storing the text on chain would make writing it expensive and checking it unnecessary. Storing the root makes checking it cheap and changing it impossible: fetch the bytes, recompute, compare.

Creation is gated; trading is not. A market needs a curator's signature. Buying and selling need only a wallet — the registration gate exists in the contract but is switched off on this deployment.
